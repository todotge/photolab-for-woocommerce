Features

Everything You Need to Sell Photos

Photolab handles the entire pipeline — from upload to watermark to WooCommerce product — so you don't have to.

Bulk Upload, Zero Friction

Upload 2,000+ photos per album in chunks of 5. SHA-256 deduplication catches duplicates before they hit your server.

Automatic Watermarking

Imagick-powered compositing with GD fallback. Full-width or corner positions. Originals stay full resolution, protected.

WooCommerce Native Products

Each photo becomes a Simple, Virtual, Downloadable product. Categories per album. Featured images set. Downloads point to originals.

Protected Originals, Secure Delivery

.htaccess denies direct access to originals. Download Guard blocks purchases until watermarked. Full-resolution only after payment.

Self-Healing Pipeline

Finite State Machine with CAS prevents race conditions. Stuck uploads auto-abort. Failed watermarks retry. Recovery cron every 5 minutes.

Multi-Domain Centralized

Manage galleries across unlimited WordPress sites from one dashboard. Remote upload, delete, reset. Native REST API, no satellite modifications.
